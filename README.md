# Lambo01 Master

Lambo01 lets you browse the web on one device and have every other device in your group follow along, in real time.

One device acts as the **Master** and controls the session. The other devices act as **Slaves** and automatically copy what the Master does. Open a website on the Master, and the Slaves open it too. Tap, scroll, type or navigate on the Master, and the Slaves do the same.

## Key Features

- **Synchronised browsing**: Pages opened on the Master are opened on every Slave in the same room.
- **Mirrored actions**: Taps, scrolling, typing, going back, going forward and reloading are all repeated on the Slaves.
- **Master and Slave roles**: Each device is given a room and a role. Only the Master controls the session; Slaves simply follow.
- **Late joiners catch up**: A device that joins late, or reconnects after losing its connection, jumps straight to the page the Master is currently on.
- **Websites ready to browse**: Your list of websites is shown on the home screen with their icons. Tap **Browse** to open one.
- **Per-device sign-in shortcuts**: Type `_username_` or `_password_` into a field on the Master, and each device fills in its own assigned login for that website.
- **Proxy support**: Devices can be set up to reach a website through an assigned proxy (Android only).
- **Always-on screen**: The screen stays awake while browsing so sessions are never interrupted.
- **Live feedback**: Small notifications show when actions are being replicated, and a banner tells you if the connection drops.
- **Secure access**: Signing in is required. Each user only sees their own devices, websites and settings.

## How It Works

1. **Sign in** with your account.
2. Your device is registered automatically and shown in **Settings**, along with its room, role, proxy and login status.
3. Your administrator assigns each device to a **room** and a **role** (Master or Slave).
4. On the **Master**, choose a website from the home screen and tap **Browse**.
5. Every **Slave** in the room opens the same page and mirrors everything the Master does.

## Screens

- **Home (Master)**: Your websites, shown as cards you can tap to browse.
- **Home (Slave)**: Shows the connection status while waiting for the Master to start a session.
- **Browser**: The synchronised web view, with back, forward and reload controls.
- **Settings**: A read-only overview of this device, its room, role, connection status, proxy and login assignment, with a refresh option and a log-out button.
- **Profile**: Shows your account email and lets you log out.
- **About**: This page, with release notes.

## Tips

- Pull down on the home or settings screen to refresh.
- If a device says it is awaiting a room assignment, ask your administrator to assign it.
- If the connection drops, the app reconnects on its own and catches up with the Master.
- Room and role assignments cannot be changed from inside the app.

## Requirements

- An Android device (some features, such as proxy support, are Android only).
- An account with access to the service.
- An internet connection.
